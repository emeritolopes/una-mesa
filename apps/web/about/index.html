About Una Mesa

One table at a time.

“A booking should feel like the start of the meal, not a form you fill in.”

Una Mesa is a reservation platform for London’s independent restaurants. You watch short videos of the dishes before you choose, book in a few taps, and hold your table with a small deposit that goes straight to the restaurant. Cancel more than 24 hours ahead and it’s released in full.

A note from the founders

Why “Una Mesa”?

restaurant

In Spanish, una mesa simply means a table. Not a cover count or a slot in a spreadsheet, but the place where people sit down, order too much bread and stay longer than they planned. That’s what we wanted a booking platform to protect.

We kept hearing the same two stories. Diners picking a restaurant from a few photos and a star rating, never quite sure what would arrive at the table. And independent restaurants losing whole tables to people who booked and never came, with very little they could do about it.

So we built the two things we wished existed. A video menu, so you can see the food before you go. We film it in the kitchen ourselves, and a proper shoot takes a few hours, not a quick snap. And a small refundable deposit, so a reservation means something on both sides of the pass.

We’re starting small and in the open: London first, independent kitchens of every cuisine, Spain next. Pull up a chair.

Carlos Alberto Martínez Escobar & Emerito Lopes Vieira
